# Content Generation Prompt Library

Use these prompts with Google AI Studio, Gemini, ChatGPT, or similar AI tools to speed up your workflow.

## 1. Ideation & Brainstorming
- **Prompt:** "I run a YouTube channel about [Your Niche/Topic]. Brainstorm 10 highly engaging video ideas that are currently trending or solve common problems for [Target Audience]."
- **Prompt:** "Give me 5 unconventional or contrarian video topics related to [Topic]."

## 2. Title & Thumbnail Generation
- **Prompt:** "I am making a video about [Video Topic]. Generate 10 catchy, click-worthy YouTube titles. Include a mix of 'How-to', 'Listicles', and curiosity-gap styles."
- **Prompt:** "Based on the title '[Selected Title]', suggest 3 visually striking YouTube thumbnail ideas that create intrigue."

## 3. SEO & Descriptions
- **Prompt:** "Write a YouTube video description for a video titled '[Title]'. It should include an engaging intro, a summary of key points, relevant timestamps, and a call to action. Optimize it for the keywords: [Keyword 1, Keyword 2, Keyword 3]."
- **Prompt:** "Generate a list of 20 relevant YouTube tags for a video about [Topic]."

## 4. Script Outlining
- **Prompt:** "Create a detailed outline for a YouTube video titled '[Title]'. The video should be roughly [Length] minutes long. Include sections for a hook, intro, 3-4 main points, and a conclusion with a call to action."

## 5. Script Drafting (Iterative)
- **Prompt:** "Write the hook (first 15-30 seconds) for a YouTube video about [Topic]. The hook needs to immediately grab attention, state the value proposition, and make the viewer want to keep watching."
- **Prompt:** "Expand on this point for my video script: '[Specific Outline Point]'. Keep the tone [Tone: conversational, educational, etc.] and aim for about [Word Count] words."
